# E02 — Music theory engine: scales, modes & rhythm

## Outcome

A user can choose **how** numbers become music — scale (major, minor, pentatonics, blues…), mode
(Ionian … Locrian, including Lydian), root note, octave range, mapping strategy, rhythm and tempo,
and instrument — hear the difference immediately, and see _why_ it is math (intervals as ratios,
modes as rotations). Every choice is captured in a versioned, shareable `CompositionConfig`.
