# S03.8.2 — Spot and label coincidental chords

**As a** listener **I want** chords that form by chance to be named **so that** I notice when π
"harmonises itself".

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] When a note starts, the notes still sounding (by their written lengths) are identified as a
      chord if possible:
  - triads: major, minor, dim, aug, sus2, sus4
  - sevenths: 7, maj7, m7, m7♭5, dim7, m(maj7)
  - sixths and add9
  - inversions as slash chords
- [x] Chord symbols appear above the staff, with a highlight band; chord noteheads get a gold ring
- [x] "Last chord (by coincidence)" readout in the stats panel and in the video caption
- [x] `core/music/chords.ts` unit-tested, including bass-root disambiguation (C6 vs Am7/C)

## Notes

Detection uses _written_ note lengths. The Original preset's mono synth cuts notes off, so its
"chords" are notated overlaps rather than sounding ones. Every polyphonic preset sounds them.
