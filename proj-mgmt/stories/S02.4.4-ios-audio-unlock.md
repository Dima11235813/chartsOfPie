# S02.4.4 — Reliable audio on iOS and Android

**As a** phone user **I want** sound to work even with the ringer switch off **so that** the app
isn't silently broken on my device.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Verified on a real iPhone (ringer on/off) and Android device
- [ ] If Web Audio is muted by the ringer switch, unlock playback through a silent HTML `<audio>` element on the first tap
- [ ] Piano samples load on mobile networks with a loading indicator

- [ ] T2 — unlock workaround if needed
