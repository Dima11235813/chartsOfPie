# S03.8.1 — Scrolling grand staff

**As a** learner **I want** to see the notes on a staff as they play **so that** I can connect the
digits, the sound and written music.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] "Sheet music" view: a grand staff (treble + bass clefs) scrolls right to left with a playhead.
      Notation is proportional (space = time), so the original's random timing is shown honestly.
- [x] Noteheads use the digit colour and have accidentals and ledger lines. A bar shows each note's
      length; notes that are sounding are outlined.
- [x] The canvas has an `aria-label` summary (latest notes, last chord)
- [x] Geometry (`viz/staff.ts`) unit-tested; e2e checks it renders on 3 viewports
