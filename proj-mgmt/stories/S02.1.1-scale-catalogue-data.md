# S02.1.1 — Scale & mode catalogue in the core

**As a** musician or learner **I want** a catalogue of scales and modes **so that** π can be heard in many musical colours.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Catalogue includes: major (Ionian), natural minor (Aeolian), Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian, major & minor pentatonic, blues, harmonic minor, melodic minor, whole tone, chromatic
- [ ] The seven diatonic modes are derived by rotating the major scale, and tests confirm each against its textbook spelling
- [ ] Every scale has id, name, intervals, family and a one-line character description (e.g. Lydian: “bright, dreamy — raised 4th”)
- [ ] Legacy mapping unchanged and its tests still pass
