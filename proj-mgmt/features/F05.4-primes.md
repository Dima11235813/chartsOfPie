# F05.4 — Primes

## Why

Primes look random but are not: their last digits are only 1, 3, 7, 9, and consecutive primes
avoid repeating a last digit (Lemke Oliver & Soundararajan, 2016), which the digit ring already
draws. Written out they form the Copeland–Erdős constant.
