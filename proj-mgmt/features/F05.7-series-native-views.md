# F05.7 — Series-native views and rhythms

## Why

Some of the most beautiful pictures belong to one series only. They use the shared renderer
registry and declare which sources they support (F05.5 `supports`).

## Stories

- [ ] S05.7.1 — Ratio Fₙ₊₁/Fₙ converging on φ, alternating above and below
- [x] S05.7.2 — Fibonacci squares and golden spiral: the **Golden rectangle** view (any number).
      Digits fill the squares 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8… from the centre outwards (Fₖ × Fₖ₊₁ cells, since
      1² + 1² + … + Fₖ² = Fₖ·Fₖ₊₁), squares outlined and the spiral drawn through those reached;
      live, one more square when the digits outgrow it, turned to the frame; posters show whole
      squares only (e.g. 1,870 of 2,000 digits, said in the caption). **Spiral** toggle (on by
      default, `viewOptions.golden.spiral`, additive). Pure geometry in `viz/golden.ts` with
      tests; smoke e2e on three viewports, Spiral off and back on while paused (rule 9), poster
      baseline `golden`
- [ ] S05.7.3 — Ulam spiral and Sacks spiral from the prime indicator
- [ ] S05.7.4 — Pisano-period loop indicator for periodic readings
- [ ] S05.7.5 — Fibonacci-word and Zeckendorf rhythms (new `rhythm` ids, additive)
- [x] S05.7.7 — **Place-value wall** (R-013 L2, any number): one row per term, right-aligned so
      equal places stand in columns (`viz/placeValue.ts`, unit-tested: base-12 units and twelves
      columns loop every 24 rows, every 12th row ends 00, a window starting mid-number keeps true
      places). Constants and one-symbol-per-term loops make a triangle (row r holds r + 1 digits).
      Cells become bricks up to 3:1 for sequences, so the slow 0.2-digit-per-row staircase fills
      the frame. Poster baselines `wall`, `fibonacci-concat-wall`, `fibonacci-base-12-wall`; smoke
      e2e on three viewports; Learn article `view-wall`
- [ ] S05.7.6 — Hand-off to E04 lessons (sunflowers and Fibonacci; why primes end in 1, 3, 7, 9; the
      last-digit bias)
