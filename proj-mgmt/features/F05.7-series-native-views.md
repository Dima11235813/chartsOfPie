# F05.7 — Series-native views and rhythms

## Why

Some of the most beautiful pictures belong to one series only. They use the shared renderer
registry and declare which sources they support (F05.5 `supports`).

## Stories

- [ ] S05.7.1 — Ratio Fₙ₊₁/Fₙ converging on φ, alternating above and below
- [x] S05.7.2 — Fibonacci squares and golden spiral: the **Golden rectangle** view (any number).
      Digits fill the squares 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8… from the centre outwards (Fₖ × Fₖ₊₁ cells, since
      1² + 1² + … + Fₖ² = Fₖ·Fₖ₊₁), squares outlined and the spiral drawn through those reached;

      default, `viewOptions.golden.spiral`, additive). Pure geometry in `viz/golden.ts` with
      tests; smoke e2e on three viewports, Spiral off and back on while paused (rule 9), poster
      baseline `golden`
