# F03.13 — Hilbert carpet

All 1,000,001 digits, one cell each along a Hilbert curve (1024² = 1,048,576 cells), so digits that
are neighbours in π stay neighbours in the picture.

- [x] Live: the whole carpet is drawn faintly (the "ghost") and digits light up as they play. The
      view zooms to the square being filled: the first 4ᵏ cells of a Hilbert curve always fill
      the corner square of side 2ᵏ (unit-tested). It starts at 32 × 32 big pixels and zooms out
      2× each time the square fills. Orientation never flips, because positions always come from
      the full 1024 curve.
- [x] Posters crop to the lit area (Hilbert prefixes fill rectangles). The million-digit poster
      shows the honest notch where the last 48,575 cells stay empty.
- [x] `hilbertPoint` unit-tested: U order, visits every cell once, every step goes to an
      adjacent cell
- [x] 1M-digit poster ≈ 1.4 s
