# F05.2 — Fibonacci

## Why

The roadmap's next number after π. Its last digits loop every 60 terms (Pisano period), its
ratios converge on φ, and its digits written out make a new irrational-looking stream (R-004).

## Stories

- [x] S05.2.1 — (M3) Pure generators (`core/digits/integerSequences.ts`): Fₙ mod m without
      BigInt, BigInt terms for `concat`; tests pin A000045 terms, Pisano periods 3, 8, 20, 12, 60,
      24 and the 4 : 8 last-digit comb. A million digits take about 30 ms, so no worker yet
- [x] S05.2.2 — (M3) Readings `concat` (default, G-3) and `last-digit` from F₀ = 0 (D9); a
      Reading picker next to the Number picker, links `s=fibonacci` and `s=fibonacci.last-digit`.
      **Term starts moved to F2** (R-012 §4) with their first users: Jump to Fₙ, Benford chart,
      typographic marks, accents
- [ ] S05.2.3 — (F2) Optional accent on term starts (additive `CompositionConfig` field, default
      off), with term starts
- [ ] S05.2.4 — (M4) Fibonacci mod 12 → a 24-step chromatic loop (needs F05.5)
- [ ] S05.2.5 — (M5) Ratio Fₙ₊₁/Fₙ → φ chart and Fibonacci squares/spiral (in F05.7)

## Notes

Owner decisions (R-012): the picker defaults to **concatenated digits** with "last digit (60-step
loop)" second (G-3); positions are stored as symbol indices and shown as terms ("F₁₀₀", a Jump to
Fₙ box) or as "step k of 60" for last digits (G-4). R-012 §2 lists the affordances: Pisano width
chips in the mosaic, the census plateau message, typographic term marks and a first-digit
(Benford) chart.

R-011 §1 has the measurements; R-004 has the musical ideas.
