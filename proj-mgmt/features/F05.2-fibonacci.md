# F05.2 — Fibonacci

## Why

The roadmap's next number after π. Its last digits loop every 60 terms (Pisano period), its
ratios converge on φ, and its digits written out make a new irrational-looking stream (R-004).

## Stories

- [x] S05.2.1 — (M3) Pure generators (`core/series/fibonacci.ts`): BigInt terms for `concat`
      (1,000,000 digits from 3,094 terms in ~35 ms, so no worker), a 60-entry table for
      `last-digit` (~7 ms); tests pin A000045 terms, the Pisano period 60 (and no shorter), the
      4 : 8 last-digit comb, ~10 % per digit overall and Benford's 30.1 % first-digit ones
- [x] S05.2.2 — (M3) Readings `concat` (default, G-3) and `last-digit` (F₀ = 0, D9), each with a
      term index (`SymbolSource.terms`: start of term k, term of a position; windows keep it);
      Number picker gains **Fibonacci numbers** and a **Reading** picker ("read as All digits /
      Last digit") shown only for series with several readings; links `s=fibonacci` and
      `s=fibonacci.last-digit`; positions are called "digit" instead of "decimal place" (series
      `place`); additive ids, schema snapshots updated with them; e2e switches Fibonacci, both
      readings and back to π while paused (rule 9)
- [x] S05.2.6 — (M3) Fibonacci affordances (R-012 §2, G-4): - positions as terms: a **Term** readout ("in F₁₀₀", or "F₁₂₃ · step 4 of 60" for last
      digits), "Starting at digit 1,051 (in F₁₀₀)", and **Jump to Fₙ** in Where in Fibonacci - **Loop** width chips in the mosaic for periodic readings: the widths ≥ 12 that divide
      the period (12, 15, 20, 30, 60), `aria-pressed`; the shapes card says the shapes stop
      changing after one loop - typographic term marks (a thin rule before each term), and no decimal point for
      whole-number sequences or after a jump (bug B-021) - a first-digit table beside the digit table, against Benford's law (all-digits reading) - `SymbolSource.period`; helpers `termName`, `describePosition`, `firstDigitCounts`,
      `benfordShare`, `loopColumnWidths`, `decimalPointCell`, all unit-tested; e2e jumps to
      F₁₀₀ (stream 354224848179261915075) and F₁₂₃, checks the Benford table and the loop
      chips on and off while paused (rule 9)
- [ ] S05.2.3 — (M3) Optional accent on term starts (additive `CompositionConfig` field, default off)
- [ ] S05.2.4 — (M4) Fibonacci mod 12 → a 24-step chromatic loop (needs F05.5)
- [ ] S05.2.5 — (M5) Ratio Fₙ₊₁/Fₙ → φ chart and Fibonacci squares/spiral (in F05.7)

## Notes

Owner decisions (R-012): the picker defaults to **concatenated digits** with "last digit (60-step
loop)" second (G-3); positions are stored as symbol indices and shown as terms ("F₁₀₀", a Jump to
Fₙ box) or as "step k of 60" for last digits (G-4). R-012 §2 lists the affordances: Pisano width
chips in the mosaic, the census plateau message, typographic term marks and a first-digit
(Benford) chart.

R-011 §1 has the measurements; R-004 has the musical ideas.
