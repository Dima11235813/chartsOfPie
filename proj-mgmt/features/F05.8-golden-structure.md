# F05.8 — Golden structure for every number

## Why

φ is more than a digit stream (F05.3): the golden angle, a φ : 1 interval, golden swing and the
Fibonacci word shape sound and pictures for **any** number, π included, with no series machinery
([R-012](../research/R-012-golden-ratio-fibonacci-integration.md) §3, decision G-2).

## Stories

- [x] S05.8.1 — Golden slice: golden swing button (swing = √5 − 2 = 1/φ³, long : short = φ;
      a value of the existing field, no schema change); string art "Golden ratios" (k steps
      through 2/1, 3/2, 5/3… closing in on φ); harmonograph "Golden ratio" (φ : 1, 833 ¢, never
      closes); sunflower "Spirals" (each seed joined to its two nearest earlier seeds, always
      consecutive Fibonacci numbers back; two colour families; the caption names the counts).
      Three additive `viewOptions` fields with defaults; unit tests (spiral rule against brute
      force, Fibonacci ratios alternate and converge, golden swing through the arranger); e2e on
      and off while paused for each toggle (rule 9); golden swing survives a reload
- [x] S05.8.2 — Golden-ratio rhythms, new rhythm ids (decision G-5), for every number: - **Fibonacci word** (`fibonacci-word`): letter n of 0100101001… (closed form, OEIS A003849)
      makes the note long (0) or short (1), long : short = φ; lengths φ³/(φ² + 1) and
      φ²/(φ² + 1) steps average exactly one step, so the tempo is kept. It is its own swing (grid
      swing is ignored); accented dynamics stress the long notes. - **Zeckendorf ruler** (`zeckendorf`): a note lasts the smallest term of its position's
      Zeckendorf sum, 1 2 3 1 5 1 2 8 1 2 3 1 13 … (held at most 5 steps); each value F occurs in
      a share 1/φ^(k+1). _Changed from the R-012 sketch_ (a digit's own code as hits and rests):
      that needs several attacks per digit, which the audio player, live MIDI, offline render and
      performance log (sheet music, MIDI export, chords) don't support. The ruler keeps one note
      per digit and is still purely Zeckendorf. Multi-hit patterns stay possible later. - Both depend only on the digit's position (the engine now passes it to the arranger), so
      they survive seeking, resuming and Where in π. An older app rejects a link with these ids
      and shows its "could not be read" notice — never another rhythm silently. - Presets **Golden word** (Lydian electric piano) and **Zeckendorf harp**, levelled with
      `npm run audio:render` (−14.0 and −14.4 dB gated, no clipping) with new spectrogram
      baselines; Original untouched, every other baseline unchanged. - Tests: unit (word vs substitution over 20,000 letters, φ density, no "11"; Zeckendorf sums
      for 1–1,000 with no consecutive terms; ruler sequence and shares; arranger delays for both
      and position-not-call-order); e2e plays both and switches back to Steady.
