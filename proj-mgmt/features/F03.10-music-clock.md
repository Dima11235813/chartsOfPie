# F03.10 — Music clock

The bridge between "math is music" and "math is art": pitch is a circle.

- [x] Twelve pitch classes around a circle, in chromatic order or (toggle) the **circle of
      fifths**, where the same key's notes sit together and transposition by a fifth is one step
- [x] The current scale's notes are lit on the rim and coloured by the digit that plays them; dot
      size shows how often each pitch class has sounded
- [x] Melody trail of the last 16 notes, fading with age
- [x] Notes sounding together form a polygon. When they make a chord it glows gold and the chord
      name (e.g. Am7) appears in the centre; two notes show their interval ("minor 3rd")
- [x] `clockPosition`/`clockAngle` unit-tested (fifths: C G D A E B F♯ C♯ …); e2e covers both orders

Lesson hook (E04): triads become triangles, and major vs minor are mirror images on the clock.
